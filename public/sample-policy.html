SECTION 1: DEFINITIONS

DEF-001

Pre-Existing Condition (PEC)

A Pre-Existing Condition means any disease, illness, injury, or medical condition that:

  • Was diagnosed or treated before the Policy Start Date; OR
  • For which symptoms were present before the Policy Start Date, whether or not diagnosed; OR
  • For which medical advice or treatment was sought or recommended before the Policy Start Date.

This includes conditions that were known to the Insured Person, even if not formally diagnosed by a medical practitioner.

DEF-002

Medically Necessary

Treatment is Medically Necessary when it is:

  • Consistent with the diagnosis and customary medical treatment;
  • Required for reasons other than convenience;
  • Performed in the most appropriate setting.

SECTION 2: WAITING PERIODS

WP-001

General Waiting Period

A waiting period of 30 days applies from the Policy Start Date for all illnesses. No claims for illness-related hospitalization will be payable during this period.

This does not apply to accidents.

WP-002

Specific Condition Waiting Periods

The following conditions are subject to an extended waiting period of 120 days from the Policy Start Date:

  • Cataract and eye-related conditions
  • Hernia (all types)
  • Hysterectomy and gynecological conditions
  • Joint replacements (hip, knee)
  • Tonsillectomy and adenoidectomy
  • Sinusitis and nasal conditions
  • Gallbladder and biliary tract conditions
  • Kidney stones and urinary conditions
WP-003

Maternity Waiting Period

For maternity benefits (if applicable to your plan), a waiting period of 300 days applies from the Policy Start Date.

SECTION 3: GENERAL EXCLUSIONS

EX-GEN-001

Cosmetic and Aesthetic Procedures

No coverage for:

  • Cosmetic surgery or treatment for beautification purposes
  • Plastic surgery (unless required for reconstruction following an accident)
  • Weight loss surgery and related treatments
  • Hair transplants or treatments for baldness
EX-GEN-002

Dental Treatment

No coverage for dental treatment including:

  • Routine dental care, fillings, extractions
  • Orthodontic treatment
  • Dental implants

Exception: Dental treatment required due to an accident within 24 hours of the accident is covered up to RM 5,000.

EX-GEN-003

Fertility and Reproductive

No coverage for:

  • Infertility investigations and treatment
  • In-vitro fertilization (IVF) and assisted reproduction
  • Contraception and sterilization procedures
  • Sexual dysfunction treatment
EX-GEN-004

Pre-Existing Conditions

No coverage for treatment of any Pre-Existing Condition (as defined in DEF-001) for the first 36 months of continuous coverage.

After 36 months of continuous coverage without claims related to the condition, coverage may be considered subject to underwriting review.

EX-GEN-005

Mental Health and Psychiatric

Limited coverage for mental health conditions:

  • Psychiatric hospitalization: Maximum 30 days per policy year
  • Outpatient psychiatric treatment: Not covered
  • Substance abuse and addiction treatment: Not covered
EX-GEN-006

Experimental and Unproven Treatments

No coverage for:

  • Experimental or investigational treatments
  • Treatments not approved by the Malaysian Ministry of Health
  • Clinical trials and research-related treatments

SECTION 5: BENEFIT LIMITS AND SUBLIMITS

SUB-001

Overall Annual Limit

Maximum benefit per policy year: RM 500,000

Lifetime maximum: RM 2,000,000

SUB-002

Room and Board

ICU Room RM 700 Per day (max 60 days)

If actual room charges exceed the limit, the Insured Person shall pay the difference.

SUB-003

Surgical Fees

SUB-004

Implants and Prostheses

Maximum coverage for implants and prostheses: RM 20,000 per policy year.

This includes but is not limited to:

  • Cardiac stents and pacemakers
  • Orthopaedic implants (plates, screws, joints)
  • Cochlear implants
  • Intraocular lenses
SUB-005

Benefit Limit

Post-hospitalization (within 60 days after discharge) RM 1,500 per admission
Emergency Outpatient Treatment (accident) RM 2,000 per event

SECTION 6: DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS

DOC-001

Required Documents for Claims

The following documents must be submitted for all claims:

  • Completed claim form
  • Original itemized hospital bills and receipts
  • Detailed medical report from the attending physician
  • Discharge summary
  • Copy of identification documents
DOC-002

Additional Requirements for Surgical Claims

For surgical procedures, additionally submit:

  • Surgeon's report with procedure details
  • Anaesthesia report
  • Histopathology report (if applicable)
  • Pre-operative assessment
DOC-003

Guarantee Letter (GL) Requirements

For pre-approval/Guarantee Letter requests:

  • Submit request at least 3 working days before elective admission
  • Include detailed cost estimate from the hospital
  • Provide doctor's referral letter with diagnosis
  • Emergency admissions must be notified within 24 hours

SECTION 7: PANEL AND NETWORK

NET-001

Panel Provider Benefits

Treatment at Panel Providers:

  • Cashless admission with valid GL
  • No upfront payment required (subject to policy terms)
  • Direct billing to insurer

List of Panel Providers available at www.medisure.com/panels

NET-002

Non-Panel Provider

Treatment at Non-Panel Providers:

  • Reimbursement basis only
  • Subject to 20% co-payment by the Insured
  • Claims must be submitted within 30 days of discharge
